# Design Document: Faberon v0.3.1 (drafter)

**Status:** current WIP (design review last updated 2026-10-06).
This doc describes what v0.3.1 should ship and is frozen afterwards.

## Goal

A human can draft a research plan conversationally instead of writing JSON by hand. A drafter mode in the console interviews the human and produces a plan for review and submit. 

A finished campaign can be followed up with a new campaign more easily. A follow-up starts a new campaign from the predecessor's best kept commit, either mechanically (same plan, fresh budget, no LLM) or drafted through the console when the plan should change. `list` and `show` also show live campaign progress (experiments done, GPU-hours burned, baseline to current best) derived from the ledger.

Still tested against Karpathy's [autoresearch](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch) (5-minute `train.py`, `val_bpb` metric).

## In scope

- **Drafter.** A conversational intake mode, hosted in the console, that interviews the human and drafts a `ResearchPlan`. It is a mode layered on the console's tool layer, not a standalone feature: it ends by submitting the reviewed plan through the same `create` path as a hand-written one. The drafter has no autonomous authority: its only output is a plan the human reviews and submits through `POST /v0/campaigns`. Control-plane validation stays the sole acceptance gate.
- **Succession from the best kept commit.** `POST /v0/campaigns` accepts an optional `predecessor` campaign id. The control plane derives the predecessor's best kept commit from its ledger (the `sha` of the last `experiment.judged` with `judgment=keep`) and its best kept metric, rejects a predecessor that is still active, and starts the new campaign's working tree at that commit. The new campaign's `campaign.created` payload records `predecessor: {campaign_id, sha}`.
- **Mechanical follow-up.** A `follow-up` command (CLI, and a console action on a finished campaign) derives the follow-up without an LLM: the predecessor's plan with fresh budget and `max_experiments` given explicitly, the baseline seeded from the predecessor's best kept metric, `predecessor` set, submitted through the same create path. This is the one-step form of the v0.2.1 re-seed recovery.
- **Drafter-assisted follow-up.** When the plan should change (new goal, revised stop conditions, a pivot informed by what was tried), the drafter starts from the finished campaign instead of a blank interview: it pre-fills the plan, summarizes what was tried from the event stream, and iterates with the human before submit. Same `predecessor` field underneath; control-plane validation stays the sole acceptance gate.
- **Live progress in `CampaignInfo`.** The campaign detail response carries progress derived from the ledger: experiments done, GPU-hours burned, baseline to current best. `list`/`show` and the console render campaign progress without a separate events read.

## Out of scope

Planned for later releases: see [roadmap.md](roadmap.md). Speculative ideas: see [future.md](future.md).

- Parallel experiments (worktrees, DBOS queue): v0.4.0.
- Judgment quality (confidence measure, non-regression, constraint gates), the notes file, approval gates and the `POST /v0/approvals/{id}` write path: v0.5.0. The console in this release has no approvals to act on, because no actions are gated yet.

## v0.3.1 limitations

- **Drafter plan quality.** The drafter drafts; the human reviews and edits before submitting. No autonomous plan acceptance.
- **A follow-up is a new campaign.** It does not continue the old workflow, budget, or experiment count. It is a fresh campaign whose plan and starting commit derive from its predecessor. Recovery of a still-pending workflow stays with `POST /v0/campaigns/{id}/resume` and is unchanged.
- **Mechanical follow-up needs explicit limits.** The follow-up command does not guess a budget: `budget_gpu_hours` and `max_experiments` are required inputs, since the predecessor's were consumed. The drafter-assisted path proposes them in conversation instead.

## Exit test

On the login node, with the control plane running:

- Draft a plan through the console's drafter, review it, submit it. The campaign starts.
- Let a campaign end, then run the mechanical follow-up with explicit budget and experiment limits. The follow-up starts from the predecessor's best kept commit, its `campaign.created` event names the predecessor, and its first judgment compares against the seeded baseline.
- Draft a follow-up through the drafter instead. The plan arrives pre-filled, the summary matches the ledger, and the human edits before submitting.
- `faberon list` and `faberon show <id>` render live progress: experiments done, GPU-hours burned, baseline to current best, without reading the event stream.
